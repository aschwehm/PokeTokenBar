# PokeTokenBar v0.4.0 — Feature & Gameplay Ideas Roadmap 🚀

This document preserves the creative feature proposals and gameplay concepts brainstormed for the **v0.4.0** (and beyond) milestone.

---

## 1. ⚔️ Gym Leader Battles & Sprint Boss Fights (Milestone Battles)
- **Mechanic**: Reaching major token milestones (e.g. 50M tokens today, large PRs, or sprint milestones) triggers a challenge from a Gym Leader:
  - *Brock — The Monolith Architect* (Rock/Ground)
  - *Misty — The Stream & Async Master* (Water)
  - *Lt. Surge — The High-Throughput Dynamo* (Electric)
  - *Sabrina — The Prompt Conjurer* (Psychic)
  - *Giovanni — The Legacy Code Kingpin* (Ground/Dark)
- **Developer Move Set**: Buddy automatically uses themed moves powered by token efficiency:
  - *“Swinub used Cache Hit! Critical hit!”*
  - *“Charizard used Prompt Refactor! Fixed all lint errors!”*
  - *“Gengar used Subagent Swarm! Overwhelmed the bug!”*
- **Badge Case Tab**: Win and collect authentic pixel-art Gym Badges to show on your profile.

---

## 2. ⚡ Mega Evolution & Gigantamax Overdrive (High-Burn Burst Mode)
- **Overdrive Mode**: When the token burn rate reaches the `🔥 On Fire!` or `⚡ Hyper Pace` tier during intense agentic loops, your companion enters **Mega Evolution** or **Gigantamax** mode.
- **Visual Effects**: Glowing aura particles, custom Mega sprite animations on both the main window and floating desktop pet.
- **Coin Rush**: 2× Token Coin multiplier awarded in the PokéShop while Overdrive is active.

---

## 3. 🏕️ 6-Pokémon Party & Multi-Tool Dedicated Buddies
- **Tool-Specific Buddies**: Assign specific Pokémon partners to different AI providers:
  - 🤖 **Claude Code** ➔ *Gengar*
  - 🌌 **Antigravity / Gemini** ➔ *Dragonite*
  - ⚡ **Cursor / Codex** ➔ *Pikachu*
- **Daycare Center**: Place hatched Pokémon in a passive Daycare where they slowly gain EXP from background coding tokens across all tools.

---

## 4. 🎵 8-Bit Chiptune Sound Effects & Cry Synthesizer
- **Authentic Game Boy Audio**:
  - Iconic level-up and evolution victory fanfares.
  - Official 8-bit Pokémon cries when clicking your buddy or viewing Pokédex entries.
  - Soft retro chimes when purchasing items in the PokéShop or hatching eggs.
- Full volume and mute controls in Settings.

---

## 5. 🕹️ Desktop Pet 2.0: Interactive Petting, Moods & Sleep Mode
- **Petting & Click Reactions**: Clicking, double-clicking, or hovering over the floating desktop companion makes it jump, bounce, or emit heart/sparkle particles.
- **Sleep & Wake Cycle**: After 15 minutes of coding inactivity, your pet curls up and goes to sleep with `Zzz` bubbles. The moment a new token stream begins, it perks up and wakes up.
- **Berry Feeding System**: Buy Oran / Sitrus Berries in the PokéShop to boost buddy mood and unlock special typing particle trails.

---

## 6. 🌐 Discord Rich Presence & Retro Trainer Cards
- **Discord Status**: Live integration displaying:
  - *"Coding with Swinub (Stage 1/3) • 123.4M tokens today • 🔥 On Fire!"*
- **Exportable Retro Trainer Card**: One-click generation of a pixel-art Trainer Card PNG summarizing lifetime tokens burned, favorite partner, Pokédex completion rate, badges, and custom title (*Prompt Engineer*, *Token Tycoon*, *Subagent Commander*).

---

## 7. 🧬 Branching Evolutions & Evolution Stones
- **Multi-Branch Lines**: Support for Eevee (8 Eeveelutions), Poliwhirl (Poliwrath vs Politoed), Slowpoke (Slowbro vs Slowking), Gloom (Vileplume vs Bellossom), etc.
- **Evolution Stones in PokéShop**: Purchase Fire Stones, Water Stones, Thunder Stones, Moon Stones, and Sun Stones to choose your desired branch.

---

## 8. 📅 GitHub-Style Token Heatmap & Daily Streaks
- **Contribution Matrix**: Color-graded yearly heatmap visualizing token burn intensity and evolution milestones by day.
- **Daily Coding Streaks**: Maintain active coding streaks to earn daily mystery egg bonuses and Rare Candy rewards.
